Before you press Play

  • Pillows in front and behind. The K1 cannot catch itself.
  • Close the K One app. Only one Bluetooth central can hold the robot.
  • Keep this tab in front. A background tab has its timers throttled and can fall out of sync with the narration.
  • Stop sends the stop frame, not a rescue. Watch the robot; do not start another story until it is standing and still.
  • The narration is read from the SD card. If the clips are missing, the stunts still play and the story is silent.
